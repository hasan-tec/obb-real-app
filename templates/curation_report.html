Generate Monthly Curation Report

⚡ How It Works

Takes every customer due a box this month (cancellations already excluded), works out each one's trimester for the ship date, then blocks every item that appears in a recently built kit — this trimester's kits, plus the kits they'd have received in earlier trimesters. What's left is the CAN USE pool. It also flags who's already covered by kits in stock and how many new units the warehouse needs to build.

Curating ahead? If the month you pick has no orders yet (it hasn't started), the pool automatically uses last month's customers instead, with their trimesters recalculated for this month's ship date — this month's own kit blocking still applies.
